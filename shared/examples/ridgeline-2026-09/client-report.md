# Ridgeline Injury Lawyers, Riverton office: Google Maps report

**Period:** June 18 to September 16, 2026, compared with March 19 to June 17, 2026
**Prepared by:** Daniel Goodrich, Local Service Spotlight · Reviewed September 22, 2026 · anonymized example

## Summary

Calls from the Riverton Google profile rose to 329, up from 308 (+7%). Website visits from the profile fell to 205 from 236 (−13%), and direction requests held at 180 versus 185 (−3%). In total, people took 714 actions on the profile versus 729 (−2%). These are taps on the profile's Call, Website and Directions buttons, not answered calls or signed cases.

## Are more people finding us on Maps?

Maps views came to 569 versus 292. Most of that jump is one day: August 17 recorded 183 desktop Maps views, when a normal day runs 0 to 14. Without that day, Maps views were 386, up 32% on last period. Google Search views of the profile rose to 1,976 from 1,780 (+11%). Google has not finished reporting search views for September 14 to 16, so this period's search figure should settle slightly higher.

## Are they acting?

28 of every 100 profile views turned into a call, website visit or direction request, versus 35 last period. Excluding the August 17 spike, the figure is 30. Weekly calls ranged from 15 to 41 across full weeks this period (15 to 33 last period); the busiest week began July 20.

## What people searched

July is the latest month Google has published. 21 search terms surfaced the profile, versus 18 in June, and each had fewer than 15 searches, so Google withholds exact counts. 8 of the 21 were the firm's name or misspellings of it ("ridgline lawyer", "ridgelin injury"), versus 4 of 18 in June. The rest were local searches such as "lawyers in riverton wv", "attorney riverton wv", "personal injury lawyer" and "motorcycle accident lawyer".

## Reviews

35 new reviews this period, the same as last period, all 5 stars; the profile's lifetime average is 4.9 across 526 reviews. Every review from this period has a reply, compared with 14 of 35 last period. Four 1-star reviews with no written comment, posted April 20 and 21, still have no reply.

## Opportunities

- **Photos:** the newest photo was added October 21, 2025 (102 in total). Current photos of the office and team should keep the profile looking active.
- **Posts:** the last Google post went up June 30, 78 days before the end of this period, compared with five posts in June.
- **Services that don't match the practice:** the profile description, a listed service and several service entries advertise medical malpractice, which the firm refers out. The profile appeared for "medical malpractice lawyer near me" in July. Lemon law and legal malpractice are also listed as services. Removing what the firm does not take should keep those callers from reaching the Riverton line.
